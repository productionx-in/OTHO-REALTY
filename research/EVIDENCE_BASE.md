**Compiled:** August 2026

## 2. The strategic inversion

The input document argues: *Hyderabad is booming, therefore build a platform to capture the boom.*

The data says: **Hyderabad has the worst inventory overhang in India (19 months) while continuing to launch
supply faster than it sells it.** Sales +1%, launches ~flat but already in surplus, ~97,000 unsold units,
and one flagship micro-market (Neopolis) sitting on ~40% unsold stock.

That is a *better* setup for OTHO, not a worse one:

1. **Developers are the desperate party, not the buyers.** Capital is locked in unsold towers. The scarce
   commodity is not leads — it is **conversion**. An advisory firm that can demonstrably clear inventory has
   real pricing power on mandates. OTHO should sell inventory clearance, not lead generation.
2. **Buyers face choice paralysis, not scarcity.** With ~97,000 options, the valuable service is not "find me
   a property" — it is **"tell me which of these will not destroy my capital."** That is precisely what
   Honest Negatives, True Density, Loading Factor and UDS deliver. The glut is the reason radical
   transparency works commercially, not just ethically.
3. **A buyer's market rewards the advisor and punishes the classified.** Portals monetise volume of listings.
   In a glut, volume is worthless and judgement is priceless.

## 4. Infrastructure catalysts (status-checked)

### Hyderabad Metro Phase 2 — **NOT YET FINANCIALLY SANCTIONED**
- 76.4 km, **₹24,269cr**, 50:50 GoTS–GoI joint venture.
- Telangana Cabinet approval Oct 2024; administrative sanction Nov 2024.
- As of **late May 2026**: DPR submitted and discussed at ministerial level; Centre gave **in-principle**
  approval for 50:50 funding; **full financial sanction still pending**.
- Expanded plan: **122.9 km across 8 corridors**.
- **June 2026: Supreme Court dismissed petitions against Phase 2** (removes legal risk).
- Construction has **not begun on most corridors**. Staggered opening targeted **2028–2030**.
- Priority corridors (Airport, Patancheru, Medchal) were targeted to start 2025–26.

> **Implication:** any "Metro Phase 2 ROI map" must display funding status, construction status and a
> probability band. Presenting it as committed infrastructure is the exact opacity OTHO claims to oppose.

### Bharat Future City (Mucherla) — **live, and the content window is now**
- FCDA operational; land acquisition largely complete across several mandals.
- Additional **301 acres** notification issued **29 May 2026**; compensation up to **₹82 lakh/acre**.
- Global tenders for master plan floated Feb 2026; bids opened **16 March 2026**.
- **Master plan + zoning regulations + Development Control Rules due December 2026.**
- **80+ private layout applications** already filed with FCDA as of mid-2026.
- **AWS hyperscale data centre foundation stone laid 15 July 2026.** 300 acres allocated to Amazon
  (202 acres in Bharat Future City + 98 acres in Chandanvelly).
- STT GDC India MoU with Telangana: **USD 412 mn** data centre at Mucherla.
- Note the rebrand: the project is now **"Bharat Future City"**, not "Future City". Content and SEO must
  target both.

> **Implication:** the December 2026 master plan release is a rare, dateable, high-search-volume event.
> Whoever owns the explainer content going into it owns the category. This is OTHO's single best organic
> land-grab and it has a deadline.

## 5. Regulatory constraints (binding, not optional)

### TG-RERA
- Real estate agent registration **mandatory**; valid 5 years; certificate number must be quoted in
  transactions and advertisements.
- Penalty for facilitating without registration: **₹10,000/day**, or up to **5% of project cost**.
- Projects >500 sq m **or** >8 apartments must be registered **before** marketing or sale.
- **RERA registration number is mandatory in every advertisement** — print, digital and outdoor.
- Enforcement is live: TG-RERA has issued notices to developers (including Godrej, Navanami, Vertex) for
  misleading and pre-launch advertising, and has imposed **~₹10cr in penalties**.

### TRAI — TCCCPR 2nd Amendment (12 Feb 2025)
- Commercial calls permitted **09:00–21:00 only**.
- Complaint processing window cut **30 days → 5 days**; complaint threshold cut **10 → 5**.
- **Explicit consent validity capped at 7 days** for commercial transactions.
- Penalties: **₹1 lakh** first violation, **₹2 lakh** subsequent.
- Enforcement scale in 2025: **731,000+ notices** to unregistered telemarketers; **560,000+ entities**
  restricted; **~90,000 repeat offenders** banned up to 6 months.
- Real estate promoters are named in scope.

### DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025
- Rules notified **13 Nov 2025**, with an **18-month runway** — core obligations (notice, consent, breach
  reporting, data-principal rights workflows) due by **~mid-May 2027**.
- Consent must be **free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous**, via clear affirmative action.
- Notice must be **standalone**, plain-language, collected **separately from T&Cs**.
- **Withdrawal must be as easy as granting.** Consent dashboards effectively required.
- Applies to any business processing digital personal data in India.

> **Implication:** the legacy portal model — capture a lead, resell it to 8 brokers, let them cold-call —
> is becoming legally expensive. OTHO's "your number is never shared" promise is not just marketing; it is
> the compliant architecture, and it is provable. Build the compliance story into the brand.

## 7. Competitive & comparable economics

| Company | Revenue | Margin / note |
|---|---|---|
| Anarock (group) | **₹946cr FY26** (+25%); ₹755cr FY25 (+33%) | Full-stack advisory + mandates; the model OTHO is emulating |
| Square Yards | **₹1,410cr FY25** | **EBITDA ₹46cr = 3.3%** — volume brokerage is low-margin |
| PropEquity | **₹50.7cr** (FY Mar 2025) | ~45% EBITDA; 1,41,327 projects / 44,967 developers / 44+ cities |
| NoBroker | — | ~10mn monthly visitors; zero-brokerage disruption model |

**Channel economics:** developer commissions to channel partners run **1–3%** of property value, up to **5%
for luxury**. Roughly **70% of residential sales in India flow through channel partners.**

**The margin lesson:** Square Yards proves you can build ₹1,400cr of revenue in Indian PropTech and keep
almost none of it. Anarock proves that advisory + exclusive mandates compounds. PropEquity proves data is
high-margin but small. OTHO's target mix should therefore be **mandate-led revenue with a data annuity
attached — and an explicit refusal to chase GMV.**
