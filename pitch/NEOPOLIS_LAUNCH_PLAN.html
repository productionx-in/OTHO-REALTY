01

Where Neopolis stands

Three numbers from published market reporting. You'll know better than anyone whether they match what you're seeing on the ground.

40% of roughly 10,000 homes launched in Neopolis are still unsold Trade press, 2026
₹6,300 per sq ft being quoted by agents, against a list price of ₹11,000–12,000 Trade press, 2026
19 months of unsold inventory in Hyderabad — the highest of any major Indian city Anarock

When agents are quoting half the list price, the project's price is being set by whoever needs the sale most that week. Buyers stop believing the advertised number. Anyone who paid full price feels cheated. And the developer loses control of how the project is described.

Discounting is what fills the gap when demand isn't being created.

That's the job. Build enough direct, tracked demand that OTHO isn't negotiating from a weak position — and own the buyer relationship instead of renting it from a portal.

Please correct this if it's wrong

These figures cover the Neopolis micro-market generally, not OTHO's own inventory. If the picture on your side is different, tell us — the plan changes with it.

Five rules we build to

Phone first

Most of this traffic arrives on a phone, often on a weak connection. The site is designed for that screen first and tested on real 4G, not on office wifi.

Nothing hidden behind a form

Price, floor plans and location are visible without giving a number. Gating them loses far more buyers than it captures.

Loads in under three seconds

Every second of delay costs enquiries. Images compressed, video streamed, page weight watched every week.

One clear action per screen

Book a visit, download the plan, or start a WhatsApp chat. Never all three competing for attention.

Written for a nervous buyer

Someone spending three crore is looking for reasons to trust you. Straight answers do more than adjectives.

RERA number on every page

Required in all advertising. Built into the template so it can't be forgotten.

Read this row before anything else

The gap between losing money and making three times the spend is conversion, not media budget. Same ₹8L. The difference is how fast enquiries get answered, how good the site visit is, and how well the website filters serious buyers from browsers.

That's where we'd put the effort, and it's why response time is reported weekly rather than monthly.

Bookings also pay on registration, not on the day of sale, so cash lands three to six months behind the marketing. Worth planning the working capital around.

What we'll commit to

At day 30 we bring real numbers from the actual funnel and set targets against them together. At day 90 you can see whether the pattern is worth copying to the next project. If it isn't working, we'll say so plainly rather than reframe it.

07

180 days

Six months, in thirty-day blocks. Each one has something finished at the end of it.

Days 1–30Setup

Get the plumbing right

  • Collect every existing asset — renders, plans, approvals, old campaign data
  • Access to ad accounts, analytics, portals, social handles
  • Sit with the sales team: what do buyers ask, where do they walk away
  • Record today's baseline — enquiries, sources, response times, conversion
  • CRM set up with routing and a response clock. Tracking built and tested
  • Website design signed off. Launch shoot planned
You get: a written baseline of where things stand today, and a working CRM
Days 31–60Build

Website and first content

  • Website built and live — all nine pages, booking system connected to CRM
  • Launch shoot completed; first films and cutdowns edited
  • Messaging and creative direction approved
  • Google Search and portals go live — existing demand first, fastest feedback
  • Compliance check on every ad before it runs
You get: a live website, the first film, and the first tracked enquiries
Days 61–90Open up

All channels running

  • Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn and Display live
  • Retargeting switched on now there's an audience to retarget
  • Weekly content publishing begins
  • Channel partner kit issued with per-partner tracking links
  • First full funnel report using our own numbers — targets set here
You get: real cost per enquiry and cost per booking, and agreed targets
Days 91–120Tighten

Cut what's not working

  • Budget moved between channels on 90 days of evidence
  • Creative testing at volume; weak performers retired
  • Website changes based on where people actually drop off
  • Site visit experience reviewed with sales — the highest-value fix available
  • Performance Max added now that conversion tracking is trustworthy
You get: lower cost per booking than month three
Days 121–150Compound

Free traffic starts arriving

  • Search and AI citations begin producing enquiries at no media cost
  • Referral programme live with existing buyers
  • Testimonials filmed and pushed into every channel
  • PR cycle running on construction milestones
You get: a growing share of enquiries that cost nothing per lead
Days 151–180Package

Turn it into a template

  • Everything documented — what worked, what failed, what it cost
  • Website rebuilt as a reusable template for the next project
  • Creative formats, ad structures and reporting packaged
  • Six-month review, and the plan for project two
You get: a system, not just a campaign
